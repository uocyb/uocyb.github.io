Welcome to the University of Oregon

CYBERSECURITY CLUB!

>whois cybersecurity-club

We are a group of UO students who are interested in and geared towards advancing student knowledge in all elements of cybersecurity. We have several events that we take part in at and around the University. Below are just a few of the things we focus on at the Cybersecurity Club.

>cat member-benefits

What you get as a member

Thursday teaches the craft in the room. Tuesday puts reps on the board. Membership is both — those two meetings are how you unlock the benefits below.

Hack The Box licenses

Hands-on practice on real boxes — used in Tuesday reps and Thursday labs.

Red and blue team experience

Offense and defense, not just slides — CTF lanes, workshops, and competition prep.

ISC² mentoring

Mentoring toward certs and career paths through club meetings and officer support.

TSOC Co-op connections

Introductions into real security operations — relationships you build by showing up.

Internships & full-time doors

Pipelines through club partners and alumni: conferences, industry guests, and a portfolio from both tracks.

>how-you-get-it

Show up Thursday to learn the shape. Stay for Tuesday if you want the hours.

Thursday · public club

PSC B040 · 5:45–6:45 — Brief, wins, calendar, then a topic. Open room.

Tuesday · competitive

Discord voice + screenshare — solve, get faster. Competitive-crew cadence.

Conferences & summits

BSides, OCRS, industry events — posted on the calendar, prepped in both weekly tracks.
