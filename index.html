Welcome to the University of Oregon

CYBERSECURITY CLUB!

>whois cybersecurity-club

We are a group of UO students who are interested in and geared towards advancing student knowledge in all elements of cybersecurity. We have several events that we take part in at and around the University. Below are just a few of the things we focus on at the Cybersecurity Club.

Thursday Public Club

Workshops, speakers, and hands-on labs in Allan Price Science Commons (PSC B040), 5:45–6:45 PM. This is where most member benefits show up week to week.

Tuesday Competitive Track

Discord voice + screenshare for CTF reps with the competitive crew. Low ceremony, high practice — the other half of how you cash in club benefits.

Conferences & Summits

BSides, OCRS, industry summits, and more. We post dates on the calendar and prep for them in Thursday club and Tuesday practice.

>cat member-benefits

Membership is not a mailing list — it is a weekly interface. Show up Thursday for taught + social club, and Tuesday for competitive reps. Those two tracks are how you unlock the benefits below.

HackTheBox licenses

Member access to HackTheBox so Tuesday practice and Thursday labs run on real boxes, not slideware.

Red / Blue team experience

Offensive and defensive reps through CTF lanes, workshops, and competition prep — built in both weekly tracks.

ISC² mentoring

Mentoring toward professional certs and career framing, connected through club meetings and officer support.

TSOC co-op connections

Pathways and introductions toward the Threat Security Operations Center co-op network — relationships you build by showing up.

Internships & full-time doors

Professional entrances into internships and full-time roles: conference trips, industry guests, and a portfolio from Tuesday and Thursday.

>locate cybersecurity-club

Thursday public club meets in Allan Price Science Commons, PSC B040, 5:45–6:45 PM. Tuesday competitive track meets on Discord voice + screenshare. Room and special-event details can shift during the year — check Discord and the calendar for changes.
