Thursday night, Sam from Docker joined the club over Zoom to talk about her career path. The part that stuck with a lot of us came from her own story. Sam had a unique background as an infrastructure developer, and that combination helped her start working at Docker with very little competition.

The takeaway is one you can use right now. Find a part-time job in college that mixes in an unusual way with your major. It gives you something that sets you apart when you apply for internships and full-time jobs, and it starts building your story before you graduate.

## OCRS: meeting the people behind the tools

On Wednesday, Oct 7, OCRS brought cybersecurity companies and people together in one room to meet and network. Justice got to meet SentinelOne, HackTheBox, Palo Alto Networks, CISA, and other organizations that protect us every day. Many UO students use their tools in student jobs, so it was good to put faces to the names.
